More Than Just A Drink

It's a Memory.

Before supermarkets. Before energy drinks. Before everything became instant – there was sugarcane juice.

Freshly pressed. Shared with friends. Enjoyed together under the summer sun.

The CaneHouse brings that timeless tradition to Sutton and beyond. We source the finest sugarcane, press it fresh while you watch and serve it with love.
