FreshSugarcaneJuiceAtSuttonSummerStreets

We pressed fresh sugarcane juice in Trinity Square at Sutton Summer Streets 2026 – here is how our summer weekends in town looked.

Home›Blogs›Fresh Sugarcane Juice At Sutton Summer Streets
Our Events2 min read

This summer The CaneHouse joined Sutton Summer Streets, the free weekend street-food markets in Trinity Square, right in the middle of our home town. We set up our stall and pressed fresh sugarcane juice for shoppers, families and everyone passing through.

What Sutton Summer Streets is

Sutton Summer Streets ran each weekend from 31 July to 6 September 2026 in Trinity Square, by Waterstones Sutton. Entry was free, and the usual hours were:

DayHours
Friday3pm – 8pm
Saturday11am – 8pm
Sunday11am – 6pm

Each weekend brought together street-food traders from Sutton and the surrounding area, along with live music, performers and free activities for children.

Our weekends in Trinity Square

We were listed among the traders on two weekends:

  • Saturday 1 August – the opening weekend, alongside Korean fried chicken, matcha, cookies and more. The same Saturday had free children's workshops, a circus skills session, a community dance class and a live covers band.
  • The August bank holiday weekend (28–31 August) – the last big weekend of the summer, when the street food joined the Northend Market on Saturday 29 August, with craft, gift and vintage stalls, face painting and live music.

What we served

Everything at our stall is pressed to order: the cane goes through the press and the juice goes straight into your cup. All the sweetness comes from the cane itself. Alongside the classic glass we serve flavours such as:

  • Mint – cool and refreshing, made for hot summer days
  • Lemon & Ginger – a fresh twist with a little kick
  • Pineapple and Watermelon – light, fruity coolers
  • Masala – an Indian twist with a spiced finish

Want to know more about the drink itself? Read all about cane.

Thank you, Sutton

Being part of a free, family-friendly event in our own town meant a lot to us. Thank you to everyone who stopped by the stall, tried a glass of fresh sugarcane juice for the first time or came back for another, and to the other traders and performers who made Trinity Square such a lively place this summer.

Where to find us next

Summer may be over, but the press keeps turning. See where our stall will be next on the events page – or, if you would like fresh sugarcane juice at your own celebration, get in touch and tell us about your event.
