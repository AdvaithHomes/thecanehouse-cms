SugarcaneJuiceForCorporateEventsAndTeamDays

A live sugarcane juice stall is a fresh, alcohol-free treat for staff days, launches and office events across Sutton, London and Surrey.

Home›Blogs›Sugarcane Juice For Corporate Events And Team Days
Occasions1 min read

Planning a staff summer day, a product launch or a celebration at the office? A live sugarcane juice stall gives your team something fresh and a little unexpected – a drink pressed to order, right in front of them.

A treat the whole team can enjoy

Sugarcane juice is alcohol-free, so it works for every colleague and every time of day, from a morning welcome to an afternoon break. All the sweetness comes from the cane itself, and with flavours such as mint, lemon & ginger, pineapple and watermelon there is something for every taste.

The live pressing is part of the fun too. People gather round to watch the cane go through the press, which makes the stall a natural meeting point at any event.

Good fits for a juice stall

  • Staff summer days and away days
  • Product launches and open days
  • Office celebrations and festive gatherings
  • Conferences, exhibitions and fairs

Packages made to measure

We tailor every package to your guest numbers, flavours, serving hours and budget, so a small office treat and a large company day can both be done well. To get a quote, tell us the date, the place, how many people you expect and how long you would like us to serve.

Smaller events can often be arranged within a few days. For busy weekends we recommend booking 4–8 weeks ahead.

Where we come to

We are based in Sutton and serve London and Surrey. For events further away, send us the location and we will let you know.

Would your team enjoy a fresh glass of cane? Send us an enquiry or find out more about our event catering.
