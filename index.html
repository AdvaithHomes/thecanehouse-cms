Our Story

A Tradition With Deep Roots

From ancient fields to modern times, sugarcane has sweetened celebrations and brought people together for generations. At harvest festivals whole canes are shared with neighbours and chewed by children in the sun.

The CaneHouse brings that timeless tradition to Sutton and beyond. We source the finest sugarcane, press it fresh while you watch and serve it with love.

Each cane is washed and fed through the press, and the sweet green-gold juice is poured straight into your glass, perhaps with a squeeze of lemon, a little ginger or a sprig of mint. Every glass carries a little of that same feeling: something simple, fresh and shared.
