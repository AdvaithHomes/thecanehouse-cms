Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

The CaneHouse is a fresh sugarcane juice business based in Sutton, London, United Kingdom. Contact: thecanehouseuk@gmail.com, +44 777 046 1939.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, booking or franchise application we collect the details you give us:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Event or order details and your message

How we use it

We never sell your details. We only share them with services that help us run the business (for example our email and form providers).

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange orders, deliveries and events
  • To send quotes, confirmations and invoices
  • To consider franchise applications

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months, or longer where the law requires (for example accounting records).

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy (/cookie-policy) for exactly what is stored on your device.
