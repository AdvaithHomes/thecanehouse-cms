Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Orders & delivery

Fresh juice is pressed to order and is best enjoyed straight away. Delivery areas and times are confirmed when you order.

Event bookings

  • A booking is confirmed once we have agreed the details in writing and any deposit is received.
  • Please give us as much notice as possible of changes to guest numbers, timings or venue access.
  • Cancellation terms are set out in your booking confirmation.

Allergens & health

Our juices may be prepared alongside lemon, ginger, mint, pineapple and spices. Please tell us about any allergies before ordering. Sugarcane juice contains natural sugars.
